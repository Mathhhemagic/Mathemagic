Linear Equations in 1 Variable

For the SAT, it is important that you know how to solve equations with 1 variable.

For example:

3x + 4 = 10
3x + 4 - 4 = 10 - 4
3x = 6

There are a few key ideas here:
1) Make sure to move around terms in the opposite order of PEMDAS:
For example, if we have 3x + 8 = 2,
instead of dividing by 3 first,
we subtract by 8 first

2) Use the opposite operation on the number to isolate the variable
For example, if we have 2x = 4,
since 2 is being multiplied by x,
we divide by 2 on both sides

You should also make sure to know distribution:

2(x + 4)
Distribute the 2 to each of the terms
2 * x + 2 * 4
2x + 8

Let’s say we have:

3x + 8 = 2x + 4
We would need to combine like terms. Combine the “x” variables and the constants, or single numbers:
Move the 2x by subtracting by 2x on both sides:
x + 8 = 4
Then fully isolate x by subtracting by 4 on both sides:
x + 8 - 4 = 4 - 4
x = 4
Finally, remember that:
When multiplying or dividing a positive number by a positive number, the product or quotient is positive

When multiplying or dividing a negative number by a negative number, the product or quotient is positive

When multiplying or dividing a positive number by a negative number, the product or quotient is negative
