:::title
Percentages
:::

A percentage is a way of expressing a number or a ratio as a fraction of 100. It is denoted by the % symbol.

:::example
50% = 50 out of 100
:::

To calculate a percentage, divide the numerator by the denominator (the part by the whole), then multiply by 100.

:::example
⅘ = 0.8 × 100 = 80%
:::

4 parts out of the whole 5 is 80%.

Let's try a word problem:

In a bag of 25 marbles, if 21 are red, what percent of the marbles are red?

:::example
²¹⁄₂₅ = 0.84 × 100 = 84%
:::

Now, a **complementary percent** is the other percent that, when added to a given percent, equals 100%.

The complementary percent of 84% is 16%.

If the price of a product increases from $15 to $18, what is the percent increase?

We follow this equation:

(New Value − Original Value) / Original Value × 100

:::example
(18 − 15) / 15 × 100 = ³⁄₁₅ × 100 = ⅕ × 100 = 0.2 × 100 = 20% increase
:::

If the price of a product decreases from $20 to $15, what is the percent decrease?

We follow this equation:

|New Value − Original Value| / Original Value × 100

:::example
|15 − 20| / 20 × 100 = ⁵⁄₂₀ × 100 = ¼ × 100 = 0.25 × 100 = 25% decrease
:::
