:::title
Converting Between Percentages, Fractions, and Decimals
:::

For the SAT, you will need to be able to quickly convert between percentages, fractions, and decimals.

:::mini-title
Percentages to Decimals
:::

Simply take the percent and move the decimal point two places to the left.

:::example
45% = 0.45
:::

:::mini-title
Percentages to Fractions
:::

Simply take the percent and put it over 100. Then, simplify if possible.

:::example
45% = ⁴⁵⁄₁₀₀ = ⁹⁄₂₀
:::

:::mini-title
Fractions to Percentages
:::

Divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100, or multiply the denominator by a number that results in 100, multiplying the numerator by the same number.

:::example
¹¹⁄₂₀ (Multiply the numerator and denominator by 5.)
= ⁵⁵⁄₁₀₀ = 55%
:::

:::mini-title
Fractions to Decimals
:::

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

:::example
⁶⁴⁄₂₀ = 64 ÷ 20 = 3.2
:::

:::mini-title
Decimals to Percentages
:::

Multiply the decimal by 100.

:::example
0.64 × 100 = 64%
:::

:::mini-title
Decimals to Fractions
:::

Move the decimal point as many places as needed to make the number a whole number. Then, place the whole number over 10 raised to the number of places you moved the decimal. Finally, simplify if possible.

:::example
0.54 (Move the decimal two places to the right.)
54 / 10² = ⁵⁴⁄₁₀₀ = ²⁷⁄₅₀
:::
