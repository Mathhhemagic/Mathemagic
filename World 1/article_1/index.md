:::title  
Integers, Negative Numbers, Absolute Value, and PEMDAS
:::


An integer is any **whole** number, including positive numbers, negative numbers, and zero.  

Fractions and decimals are **never** integers.  

Opposite numbers are numbers that are the same distance from zero but on opposite sides of the number line.  

For example, **-4** and **4** are **opposites** because they are both **4** units from zero.  

Among negative numbers, the number closer to zero is greater. For example, -1 is greater than -10 because -1 is closer to zero on the number line.  

Subtraction can be **rewritten** as addition:


:::example 
a − b = a + (−b)
:::

:::mini-titleMultiplication and Division Rules:::
:::example  

Positive × Positive = **Positive**  

Negative × Negative = **Positive**  

Positive × Negative = **Negative**  

Positive ÷ Positive = **Positive**  

Negative ÷ Negative = **Positive**  

Positive ÷ Negative = **Negative**
:::  

:::mini-titleAbsolute Value:::  

The **absolute value** of a number is its distance from zero. 

:::example
|-5| = 5  

|4| = 4
:::  

Note: Absolute value is **always** positive or zero.  

:::mini-titlePEMDAS::: 

PEMDAS stands for Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, and Subtraction.  

Multiplication and division have **equal** priority.  

Addition and subtraction have **equal** priority.  

When two operations have the same priority, solve from left to right.  

However, this may be confusing, so just remember:  

Always solve inside **parentheses** or **brackets** first.  

Then, solve **exponents**.  

Next, solve **multiplication** and/or **division** (whichever comes first from left to right).  

Finally, solve **addition** or **subtraction** (whichever comes first from left to right).  

Look at the following expression:    
:::example
(2 + 3)² × 2
:::
First, solve inside the parentheses:
:::example
2 + 3 = 5
:::
Then, use the exponent:
:::example
5² = 25
:::
Finally, multiply by 2:
:::example
25 × 2 = 50
:::  

Let's try another:
:::example
[(4 + 2)² + 5 × 4 ÷ 2] × 2
:::
This one is a long one, so let's break it down:  

First, solve the innermost parentheses:
:::example
4 + 2 = 6
:::
Then, square the 6:
:::example
6² = 36
:::
Then, multiply 5 by 4:
:::example
5 × 4 = 20
:::
Next, divide 20 by 2:
:::example
20 ÷ 2 = 10
:::
Then, add 36 and 10:
:::example
36 + 10 = 46
:::
Finally, multiply 46 by 2:
:::example
46 × 2 = 92
:::
